# DataFaultBench v0.2.2

Final manuscript-alignment release accompanying *DataFaultBench: Selection Stability and Cross-Domain Transport of Data-Fault Interactions*.

## What changed

- Added the final publication-ready forest plot, core-result figure, and three-panel simulation figure in vector form.
- Added machine-readable source tables for the upgraded figures and the extended related-work comparison.
- Added the permanent all-versions Zenodo DOI to the README and citation metadata.
- Aligned the public artifact description with the final journal-submission package.

## What did not change

- No new dataset or domain was added.
- The frozen 55-domain manifest and all Study I records are unchanged.
- The simulation configuration, 180,000 repetitions, cross-fitting outputs, and scientific conclusions are unchanged.
- The final source-disjoint directional result remains `p = 0.131`; no external Study II outcome is included or claimed.
